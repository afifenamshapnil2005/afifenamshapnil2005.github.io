Case study · BME3200 · 2026 – present

Voice-Controlled Limb Exerciser & Newspaper Page Changer

The problem

Bedridden patients need regular limb movement, and many can't hold or turn a newspaper by themselves. Commercial rehabilitation devices are expensive and rarely hands-free.

What we built

  • Foot exerciser: carriage and foot plate on two parallel 8 mm guide rods, driven by a 12 V 37GB gear motor.
  • Hand exerciser: carriage and handle moving vertically on guide rods.
  • Newspaper page changer: two SG90 servos driving a page-turn arm and a clamp arm.
  • Lever micro limit switches for safe end-of-travel stops, plus a demonstration bed and a patient dummy with jointed knees.

My role

Co-lead of a two-person team with Afia Mubasshira Islam, supervised by Tasnim Ahmed, Assistant Professor, Dept. of BME, KUET. I did the full CAD, the electronics on an Arduino Nano, and the voice control.

Process

  • Modelled 12 printable parts (P1–P12, plus a calibration block) as parametric, fully constrained models in Fusion 360.
  • Checked every part against a 17-page construction manual before assembly and print export.
  • Cut filament use by hollowing large parts and scaling the demonstration to a desk-size build.
  • Designed for a target build cost of BDT 6,000–7,000.

Status

The design is complete and the prototype is being assembled.
